Source: public HQ MP3 previews of those sounds, not original WAV masters.
Edits: conversion to mono 44.1 kHz PCM, silence trimming, fades, normalization,
and arrangement into single/double/triple reaction patterns.
Tools/AudioSources/sources.json records exact source URLs and SHA-256 hashes.

The other 30 sb_*.wav files are original procedural character voices generated
by Tools/generate_sound_pack.py. They contain no sampled speech, Apple system
voice output, copyrighted songs, film clips, or cloned human voices.
