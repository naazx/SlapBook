SlapBook Pro original synthesized character voices

All sb_*.wav files in this directory are original procedural audio, created by
Tools/generate_sound_pack.py. They contain no sampled recordings, Apple system
speech output, third-party audio, copyrighted songs, or cloned human voices.
They are stylized synthesized vocal effects, NOT recordings of human performers.
